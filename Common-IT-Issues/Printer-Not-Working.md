# Printer Not Working

## Initial Checks
- Confirm printer power, display status, and network/USB connection.
- Check whether the issue affects one user or multiple users.
- Confirm the selected printer and default printer.
- Check for paper, toner, jam, or tray errors.
- Check the print queue for stuck jobs.
- Confirm the user has permission to print.

## Windows Checks
- Open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners.
- Open the printer queue and review pending jobs.
- Check the Print Spooler service status using Services.

## Resolution
Follow the organization's printer support procedure. Do not clear shared queues or restart shared print services without checking the impact on other users. Test with a small document and confirm successful output.
